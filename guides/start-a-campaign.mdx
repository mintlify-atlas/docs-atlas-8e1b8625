title: "Set Up a New TableSage RPG Campaign and First Session"

TableSage organises your recorded games as **Campaigns**, each containing one or more **Sessions**. Before you can process a recording, TableSage needs to know who was at the table, what characters they played, and where to keep everything. This walkthrough takes you from a blank workspace all the way to a Session that is ready for processing — whether this is the very first game of a brand-new campaign or a recording from an ongoing one.

You need an installed TableSage workspace. If this is your first launch, complete [installation](/getting-started/installation) and [configure your keys and models](/guides/settings) first. Launch TableSage from the directory where you want to keep this campaign; see [Workspaces](/concepts/workspaces) for details.

<Steps>

### Open the Campaigns list

From the **Welcome** screen, press **C** to open **Campaigns**.

### Create a new campaign

Press **N** (**New Campaign**). Fill in the following fields:

- **Name** — a short, recognisable title such as *Salt Marsh*.
- **Description** — an optional sentence about the campaign's premise.
- **Game System** — the ruleset in use, for example *D&D 5e* or *Pathfinder 2e*.

Choose **Create Campaign** to save.

### Open the campaign

Select your new campaign in the list and press **E** or **Enter** to open **Campaign Detail**.

### Create the first session

On the **Sessions** tab, press **N** (**New Session**). Give the session a useful name such as *Arrival at the Harbor*, then enter its date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format — for example `2026-10-01`. Choose **Create Session**.

TableSage opens **Session Detail** for the new session. The **Attendance** list is empty; you will fill it in next.

<Note>
Use a real calendar date for every session. TableSage uses session dates to determine chronological order when it builds beginning-of-session recaps and chooses which prior-session recap to include in a summary.
</Note>

### Add attendees and roles

Every person whose voice appears in the recording must be listed in **Attendance**, including the game master.

1. Press **Tab** to move focus to the **Attendance** table, then press **N** (**New Attendee**).
2. In the **Player** combo box, select an existing person or choose **\<New player…\>** and type their real-world name. TableSage creates the player record and returns you to the attendee dialog. Use full names with normal capitalisation so players who share a first name stay distinct.
3. Add the player's role:
   - Press **R** (**Add Role**) to enter a character name.
   - Press **G** (**Add Game Master**) to assign the GM role.
4. Choose **Save**, then repeat for each additional attendee.

For example: *Jordan Lee* is the player and *Brother Hald* is their role. *Morgan Reyes* is the GM. A single player can hold more than one role in a session.

**Save** becomes available once you have selected a player and supplied at least one role.

<Note>
The **Samples** column in the Attendance table shows how many voice samples each player's voice print was built from. A red **0** means the player has no voice print yet — this is expected for anyone joining TableSage for the first time. Their voice will be learned from this recording during processing.
</Note>

### Check attendance before continuing

Before moving on, confirm that:

- Every person who spoke in the recording is listed.
- Character names are spelled correctly — these spellings flow through to every generated output.
- The GM role is assigned to the correct player.

### Optionally seed the campaign glossary

This step is optional but saves correction work later. Press **Esc** to return to **Campaign Detail**, then press **G** to open the **Glossary** tab.

Press **N** to add a name or term and an optional description. Add distinctive words you expect to hear, such as *Brother Hald* or *Tidewarden Coil*. If you prefer, you can let TableSage propose terms automatically from the recording during processing and add them then.

See [Build and Maintain Your Campaign Glossary](/guides/build-campaign-glossary) for a fuller vocabulary workflow.

### Start processing

Return to the session you created: press **S** to activate the Sessions tab, select your session, and press **Enter**. Then press **P** (**Process**) to begin.

TableSage picks the processing workflow automatically based on attendees' voice prints:

- If **any** attendee has no usable voice print, follow the [Process a Session with New Players](/guides/process-session-new-players) guide.
- If **everyone** already has a usable voice print, follow the [Process a Session with Returning Players](/guides/process-session-returning-players) guide.

</Steps>
