title: "Process a Session with Returning Players in TableSage"
sidebarTitle: "Returning Players"
description: "Process a recording when all attendees already have voice prints. Check attendance, import audio, review the transcript, and generate outputs."
---

Use this guide when every attendee already has a usable voice print. This is the normal workflow for later sessions in a campaign, but it also applies to a brand-new campaign whose players already have voice samples from prior work. If anyone's voice still needs to be learned, use [Process a Session with New Players](/guides/process-session-new-players) instead.

You need the session recording and configured keys and models. The steps below start from the Welcome screen.

<Steps>

### Open your campaign

From the **Welcome** screen, press **C** to open **Campaigns**, select your campaign, and press **E** or **Enter** to open Campaign Detail. Press **S** to select the **Sessions** tab.

### Create the session

Press **N** (**New Session**), enter a name and optional date (`YYYY-MM-DD`), and choose **Create Session**. TableSage opens Session Detail.

### Check and update attendance

TableSage automatically copies the attendee list and roles from the most recently dated session before this session's date. Review the inherited list carefully:

- Press **D** to remove anyone who was absent.
- Press **N** to add anyone who is missing.
- Press **E** to update a role that has changed — for example if a player switched characters.

Include the GM and everyone else who spoke in the recording. A player's voice samples stay the same regardless of role changes; the role simply tells generated outputs which character that person played this time.

### Start processing

Press **P** (**Process**). Confirm that the **New Players** panel is empty — if any attendee is listed there, switch to the [New Players guide](/guides/process-session-new-players) instead.

### Import the audio

Press **C** (**Continue**) and choose the recording file. Supported formats are `.wav`, `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.flac`, and `.ogg`.

When TableSage asks whether to clean the audio, you can usually choose **Yes**.

<Note>
Choose **No** only if this file was already cleaned by TableSage in a previous run. Cleaning a file that has already been processed can cause a slight degradation in audio quality.
</Note>

TableSage imports the recording, creates an initial transcript, and filters likely listener acknowledgments. Progress dialogs cover this automatic work.

### Extract glossary terms

TableSage reviews the transcript for campaign-specific jargon — NPC names, places, organisations, and other proper nouns — and shows its suggestions on the **Extract Glossary Terms** screen.

- Press **E** to edit a proposed term or its description.
- Press **D** to remove a proposal you do not want.
- Press **N** to add a term that was missed.

Choose **Continue** to add the remaining entries to the Campaign Glossary. This step may complete automatically if there is nothing to check.

### Review spelling against the glossary

Within a single transcript, the same term can appear under several spellings — for example *Deathwarden Drake* and *Death Warden Drake*. TableSage proposes replacements that consolidate variant spellings to the form recorded in the Campaign Glossary.

- Press **E** to edit a proposed replacement.
- Press **D** to toggle an incorrect replacement off.

Choose **Continue** when the list looks right. This step may also complete automatically if there is nothing to check.

### Review the transcript

**Review Transcript** is your final check of what was said and who said it. Even high-confidence speaker matches can be wrong, so check uncertain assignments and any lines whose wording could affect the record of what happened.

1. Move through the lines with the arrow keys to listen and compare audio with text. Press **Space** to toggle auto-play mode, which advances through utterances automatically.
2. To reassign a speaker, use the legend's number keys after focusing a line.
3. Press **Enter** on a line to open **Edit Utterance** and fix the text or the assigned attendee directly.
4. Press **D** to mark unwanted lines — crosstalk, off-topic speech, or audio artefacts — for removal.
5. Use **Find/Replace** to correct a term that appears many times.

Choose **Confirm** when the transcript is ready.

<Note>
If you leave the Review Transcript screen before confirming, TableSage offers to save your progress. You can return and continue from where you left off.
</Note>

### Finish generation

TableSage applies your edits, replaces player names with their roles throughout the transcript, and generates the session's outputs.

- If an earlier session needs to be rebuilt for the pre-session recap, **Prior Sessions Are Out of Date** will appear — choose **Regenerate Prior** to proceed.
- At **Improve Player Voice Prints**, choose **Add Samples** to extract voice samples from this session and strengthen player voice prints. This is especially recommended for your first few sessions.

When Process Session shows **All steps complete**, press **Esc** to return to Session Detail. The generated outputs should each show a current **●** indicator.

If a step fails, fix the cause shown on its row and press **Continue** (**C**) to retry. To revisit a completed review step, select it and press **R**; see [Correct a Processed Session](/guides/correct-processed-session) for guidance on choosing the right restart point.

</Steps>

From here you can [review and export the session outputs](/guides/review-and-export) to share with your players, or [prepare for the next session](/guides/prepare-next-session).
